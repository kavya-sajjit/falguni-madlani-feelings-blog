<p style="text-align: center;">&nbsp;MUMBAI AND RAINS!</p><p style="text-align: center;"><a href="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEjIFiq-BWGHea5Ivc-4Rruk9O-10hpFspFbGUDhjqX_DvV-DSDfF-f1-q-akoyfBQ8wgMFuoVjhPOZ5kccJfLAKdaCPALPFgUjjscsjR1PYAoGMZPr_UOQll_GVAFXu9Oz3lWVO_VFXzm2mMLQdxR9I9FTKg4-5nkbtVPfRq6v_A7s44k36xNUPfHBQXp6c/s1600/rains%202.jpg" style="clear: left; float: left; margin-bottom: 1em; margin-right: 1em;"><img border="0" data-original-height="1600" data-original-width="1200" height="325" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEjIFiq-BWGHea5Ivc-4Rruk9O-10hpFspFbGUDhjqX_DvV-DSDfF-f1-q-akoyfBQ8wgMFuoVjhPOZ5kccJfLAKdaCPALPFgUjjscsjR1PYAoGMZPr_UOQll_GVAFXu9Oz3lWVO_VFXzm2mMLQdxR9I9FTKg4-5nkbtVPfRq6v_A7s44k36xNUPfHBQXp6c/w244-h325/rains%202.jpg" width="244" /></a></p><p></p><p style="text-align: justify;">Mumbai and Rains is a match made in heaven. You should experience it to believe it, especially if you are a pluviophile.</p><p style="text-align: justify;">One day, its bright and sunny with sweat drops gleaming down your brows and the next day it is dark with rain clouds looming down the skyscrapers hiding them behind their curtains. A mist moving through the high rises is just lovely.</p><p style="text-align: justify;">There is a sea of umbrellas jostling outside the stations and hot cutting chais and kanda bhajiyas to feed the hungry souls</p><p style="text-align: justify;">The lush green SGNP is a place to behold. Every thing is just green, mystical and seems to be unreal around you. The sultry Mumbai turns into place where you feel that nature has turned on its air conditioning.&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: justify;">The Arabian sea be it at Girgaum, Marine Drive or Juhu just roars into life and puts some of it into you with its sprays of salt and sand onto you. And trust me, nothing can beat that feeling.</p><p style="text-align: justify;">The rains do bring the usual woes of late trains, long crawling traffic, empty classrooms&nbsp; in schools and colleges. But it also brings out the resilience of mumbaikars, the sharing of umbrellas, the serving of teas and Parle G to the stranded folks.</p><p style="text-align: justify;">I can go on and on of Mumbai in rains but I need to finish eating my plate of vadas and tea!</p><p style="text-align: justify;">Happy Julying in Mumbai.</p><p style="text-align: justify;"><br /></p><p style="text-align: justify;">By Falguni Madlani</p><span></span><p style="text-align: justify;">More Posts:</p><p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="https://falgunimadlani.blogspot.com/2025/10/omkar.html">Omkar</a><br /></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="https://falgunimadlani.blogspot.com/2023/09/my-favorite-ganesha-story.html" target="_blank">My favorite Ganesha story</a><br /></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="https://www.blogger.com/blog/post/edit/680146003691905224/5527213768719582065" target="_blank">Hurkle Durkle</a><br /></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><br /></p>
