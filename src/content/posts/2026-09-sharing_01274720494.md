<p>&nbsp;<b>SHARING</b></p><p>On a Sunday afternoon, I decided to do a bit of spring cleaning. So, I gathered some of my daughter's old toys and books which of course she has outgrown long back and decided to donate all of them to a nearby NGO.</p><p>But, I met with a resistance and a grumpy look as if I was doing grave injustice to her. The mother in me woke up and I just finished giving a lecture to my daughter on "The more we share, the more we get" which I have been taught right from my childhood by my Mom and Dad.</p><p>And now sitting back I am reminded of a funny incident of how children can be possessive of their things.</p><p>Me and my brother were playing outside the house for a while since morning. Mom had gone to the market to buy some things. While coming back home, she saw a poor beggar child asking for alms door to door. He was a couple of years elder to my brother. So, my mom did give some money to him to help him and also gave a set of clothes belonging to my brother to him.</p><p>Unfortunately my brother saw this. He stopped playing with me and so I went home and got busy doing something else. After some time, my mom called us for lunch and we realised that my brother was not around since some time.</p><p>She started to think where could he go without informing mom.</p><p>At that time, the child who was given my brother's shirt came back to&nbsp; our house and said "Aunty, I don't want this shirt. Wherever I am going, this boy is following me and he just folds his hands and keeps staring at me as if I have stolen his shirt. He has followed me last two hours ". Saying this he gave the shirt back to my mom who was left with no words to say. I think that was the first day of me listening to the quote "Sharing is Caring".</p><p>And coming back to my brother, he has turned out to be quite a generous person.😄</p><div>By, Falguni Madlani</div><div><br /></div><div>More Posts:</div><div><br /></div>
