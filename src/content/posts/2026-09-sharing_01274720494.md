<p>&nbsp;<b style="text-align: center;"><span style="font-size: medium;">SHARING</span></b></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: medium;">On a Sunday afternoon, I decided to do a bit of spring cleaning. So, I gathered some of my daughter's old toys and books which of course she has outgrown long back and decided to donate all of them to a nearby NGO.</span></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: medium;">But, I met with a resistance and a grumpy look as if I was doing grave injustice to her. The mother in me woke up and I just finished giving a lecture to my daughter on "The more we share, the more we get" which I have been taught right from my childhood by my Mom and Dad.</span></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: medium;">And now sitting back I am reminded of a funny incident of how children can be possessive of their things.</span></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: medium;">Me and my brother were playing outside the house for a while since morning. Mom had gone to the market to buy some things. While coming back home, she saw a poor beggar child asking for alms door to door. He was a couple of years elder to my brother. So, my mom did give some money to him to help him and also gave a set of clothes belonging to my brother to him.</span></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: medium;">Unfortunately my brother saw this. He stopped playing with me and so I went home and got busy doing something else. After some time, my mom called us for lunch and we realised that my brother was not around since some time.</span></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: medium;">She started to think where could he go without informing mom.</span></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: medium;">At that time, the child who was given my brother's shirt came back to&nbsp; our house and said "Aunty, I don't want this shirt. Wherever I am going, this boy is following me and he just folds his hands and keeps staring at me as if I have stolen his shirt. He has followed me last two hours ". Saying this he gave the shirt back to my mom who was left with no words to say. I think that was the first day of me listening to the quote "Sharing is Caring".</span></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: medium;">And coming back to my brother, he has turned out to be quite a generous person.<span style="font-family: verdana;">😄</span></span></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: medium;"><span style="font-family: verdana;"><br /></span></span></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: medium;"><span style="font-family: verdana;"><br /></span></span></p><div style="text-align: justify;"><span><span style="font-family: arial;">By, Falguni Madlani</span></span></div><div style="text-align: justify;"><span><span style="font-family: arial;"><br /></span></span></div><div style="text-align: justify;"><span><span></span><span style="font-family: arial;">More Posts:</span></span></div><div><span><span style="font-family: arial;"><br /></span></span></div>
