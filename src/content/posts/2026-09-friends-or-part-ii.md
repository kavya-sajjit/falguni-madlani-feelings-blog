Anushri started realizing the pattern which she missed over the last few weeks, the evening meets cancelled, the reduced phone calls, 'i am busy" answers.</p><p>&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;She had no idea what to do next and a a smile came to her lips on realizing the irony that heart doesn't follow rules of what should be?</p><p>&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;Then Kartik spoke " Actually, I was going to tell you all today. She will be coming now any time. And I know, Ma n pa won't refuse as it is my birthday today.</p><p>&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;At that very moment, the door bell rang. Shreya, their common friend went and opened the door and gave a shriek. Kartik's mom went to the door hurriedly and was dumbfounded.&nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;There at the door was a man standing with a desi Indie pup in a basket with a tag around her collar which read " Cookie". She just jumped into Kartik's mom hands and was all over her.</p><p>&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;Kartik took Cookie over and said "Anu hates dogs and I am crazy over them. This little one was hurt by a speeding vehicle and I have spent the last&nbsp; few weeks nursing her back.Cookie needs a home. Dogs have always been a bone of contention between us. As per Anu's ultimatum, it is either her or dogs. And, I can't leave Cookie."</p><p>The whole room broke into Ooh and Aahs. Shreya said with a laugh. "Well, that's certainly not the kind of 'someone else' we were expecting, Kartik!"&nbsp;</p><p>Cookie, unknown of the commotion she created was busy showering Kartik with kisses. Although, it was an adorable sight, Anushri was instantly reminded of her&nbsp; childhood injury due to a dog bite and the overwhelming fear of dogs.</p><p>The playful smile on Kartik's lips, the way he held Cookie made Anushri smile and say aloud "Yeah! We will remain friends at present and maybe Cookie can win me over".
