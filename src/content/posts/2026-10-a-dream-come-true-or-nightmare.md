<p style="text-align: center;">&nbsp;A Dream come true or a nightmare?</p><p>Long ago in my school days….i consider myself from an ancient era with all the creaking bones and stuff, I was given an essay to write about the topic “India in 2020”. Most of the kids in my class along with me let our imagination and ideas run wild. One of them was, we will have our private flying car.&nbsp;</p><p><br /></p><p>Fast forward to present and reading the news in the newspaper:&nbsp;<a href="http://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/articleshow/135144453.cms?utm_source=contentofinterest&amp;utm_medium=text&amp;utm_campaign=cppst" target="_blank">Flying Car by an Indian </a> Check out the news. It was like my dream came true.( Okay fine, we missed the deadline, but who’s counting?)</p><p><br /></p><p>Reading this news made me happy and proud for two reasons. First, it was my childhood dream coming true and second the person who invented it, Ravi Tamta, an Indian had no formal degree but a passion which he lives by. If you haven't seen it yet, watch it here</p><p><a href="https://youtu.be/vv4rHOk1YCI?si=H-s5Rl0THTVM4WpJ">https://youtu.be/vv4rHOk1YCI?si=H-s5Rl0THTVM4WpJ</a></p><p>Flying Car from India? Ravi Tamta</p><p>But after some time, while brooding over this news…</p><p>I was tempted to write an essay on “ Do I really want this?”🤔</p><p>The news regarding the fast pace of infrastructure development, the disregard of its effect on the environment, the weather issues, creates a concern which keeps on gnawing from within and also a helplessness that humans are showing a complete disregard for the only EARTH.</p><p>In the race for more technological advancement, humans should not lose sight of a safe and secure planet for our future generations. Ah, now that's a topic for some other blog.</p><p>But, seriously, I would miss the autowalas and taxiwalas who take so much care of my health by refusing any destination and making me walk  most days on my daily commute.</p><p>I would also miss the anger when a driver in front of me suddenly wishes to go off at a tangent on his whim and fancy. Or 🤔  would it still be the same in the sky?</p><p>But, I being the optimist I am, I surely think my smart,eco-friendly, private flying car is going to materialise soon and for the good of the world….</p><p>P.S...I really do need my private flying car😊. What about you?</p><p><br /></p><p><br /></p>
