<p style="text-align: center;">&nbsp;WHO IS AT FAULT?</p><p>The recent incident at IIT Powai where a student lost his life by suicide brought to my mind a story which was narrated by my mom when I was a child and used to sulk on being scolded. The Story is as follows:</p><p>Long ago in a small village, two families were staying beside each other. There were two five year old boys in the families who use to play whole day together. In the afternoon, they would go to school together and come back from the school by evening. One evening, while they were coming back from the school, they saw a seller selling mangoes. This seller was napping besides his spread of fruits. One of the friend named Shekhar told his friend Kunal that "we should take some mangoes, they look yummy. Since he is sleeping he will not know who has taken the mangoes." Kunal looked aghast and retorted "No! I don't think it will be right, it will be stealing na!But Shekhar instigated him and pressed further saying that "Don't be scared. Nothing will happen". Both of them took three mangoes each. On reaching home, they gave the mangoes to their mother.&nbsp;</p><p>Forty years down the lane&nbsp;Shekhar became a judge in the city court. In his court, there came a case against an income tax officer taking bribe. As soon as the parties were introduced,&nbsp; Shekhar recognized his old friend Kunal and recused himself from from the case. Shekhar met Kunal and said what have you done Kunal and What was the need for this?&nbsp;</p><p>Kunal became very embarassed&nbsp; and went home brooding. He called his mom&nbsp; and told her to come over saying "I want to talk to you urgently, please come over." Kunal's mom went to his place and asked him "what do you want to talk about beta". Kunal answered "it's a secret, I will tell it only in your ears." As soon as Kunal's mom went to hear the secret, she cried out loudly in anguish and clutched her ear. Everyone was shocked to see that Kunal had bitten his mom's ear. Kunal started sobbing and said "Mom, had you been like Shekhar's mom and reprimanded me for stealing mangoes forty years back, I would have been a different person. That evening, when I gave you the mangoes, you took that from me and used it. Shekhar's mom on the other hand threw it away and made him realize that it was wrong to steal. The next day, when we saw the napping vendor again, I told Shekhar to steal mangoes again as I had relished it without spending any money, but he refused saying that his mom was very upset. I was wondering that my mom didn't say anything to me, it may be ok stealing a bit and so I stole again. It became easy for me to take recourse to unfair means and today I have landed up in this messy situation"</p><p>So, who is at fault here in this story?</p><p>Similarly, in the incident which happened at IIT, Powai, who is at fault?</p><p>The Professor who was strict and followed the rules&nbsp;</p><p>OR the student who was embarrassed on being caught&nbsp;</p><p>OR the parents who couldn't instil values in the child</p><p>OR the reservation system in the Education field where a student with 40 % competes with a student with 99% ?</p><p>It's a question to be pondered upon and discussed in the educational Institutions.....</p><span></span><p><br /></p><p><br /></p><p></p><p>More posts:</p><p><a href="https://www.blogger.com/blog/post/edit/680146003691905224/5527213768719582065" target="_blank">Hurkle Durkle</a><br /></p><p><a href="https://madlanifalgunis.blogspot.com/2026/09/omkar.html" target="_blank">Omkar</a><br /></p>
