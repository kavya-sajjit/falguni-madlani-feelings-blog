<p>&nbsp;<b style="text-align: center;"><i>Introspection Time!</i></b></p><p style="text-align: justify;">The last few months went by in a blur. On seeing the date today, I was like "What! Its already mid December? What did I do the whole year?"</p><p style="text-align: justify;">On a customary little google research, (Ahh, it has become my habit to google things that I already know), I found out that the concept of Time is strange. Time seems to move faster as you age. Hmmm..... Now I know why I am not finding time to join a dance class.....the 24 hours of a day are just whooshing by.</p><p style="text-align: justify;">And time also races by when you are having fun. So, does that mean that the entire 2023-2024 was happy and joyful?&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: justify;">But what about the time the train to Churchgate was horribly late, driving me into panic mode during my commute to work? And the time when Mumbai was transformed into a huge black swimming pool with rains pouring from heaven. Amidst these mundane challenges, can the year be truly a joyous blur?</p><p style="text-align: justify;">But, then I also broke into a smile remembering the day I met a friend of mine after ages and we could connect in an instant like our old teenselves. The memories of hugs and love from my students, friends and family in the past year made me realize that although time is fleeting, these are the very things which leave an everlasting impressions on us.&nbsp; They make us alive and happy. They make up the very essence of us.</p><p style="text-align: justify;">So, And as the year comes to a close, let me thank everyone who was a part of my journey last year and&nbsp; made me smile. I am grateful to you all for just being there . A big Thank you!&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: justify;"><br /></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><b>By Falguni Madlani</b></p><p style="text-align: justify;"><br /><span></span></p><br />
