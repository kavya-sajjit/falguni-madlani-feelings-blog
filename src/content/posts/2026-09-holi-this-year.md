<p>&nbsp;<b style="text-align: center;"><span style="font-size: 14pt; line-height: 19.9733px;"><span style="color: #cc0000;">HOLI THIS YEAR!</span></span></b></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">It’s a Holi everyone was eagerly waiting for. After two years of being confined and social distancing, it was a Holi of get togetherness, of building memories, reliving memories and being happy.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">The preparation starts long before the festival. The days before Holi spent eagerly deciding to buy the Pichkari after discussing whether it should have more water capacity or force. And then being influenced by “last year Rhea ki pichkari se to dusre building main bhi paani fek sakte hai”.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">Choosing and buying the colours, balloons and reminiscing “hamare zamane mein silver paint aur pata nahi kya kya color lagate the”.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">On the actual day of Holi, getting ready to play Holi. Wearing the dress which needs to be discarded, white is for Bollywood na.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes;">&nbsp; </span>Then waiting eagerly for one of the friends to call us to form the gang. Doing some nakhras, “ nahi yaar, not this time while all the time thinking if they call me one more time, I will go. Then joining them and going and calling other friends and the playing the whole morning with colours and water to the beat of music.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">Playing to the heart’s fill, arranging for snacks, cold drinks are a part of this day.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">And after playing Holi coming back home, being told to directly enter the bathroom , made to scrub and remove all the colours. Coming out with traces of pink at the cheek, nape and nails. Sitting down for a sumptuous lunch of chhole puri and puran poli from neighboring aunty, after.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">This is what made our childhood and now makes up our children’s childhood. It’s important to realise the bonding, the happy hormones these gatherings create. No wonder Holi is called as the festival of hope, enthusiasm and goodness.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">These two years of Covid have made us realize the value of having friends, family and relations. The important role which they unknowingly play in our wellbeing and happiness is underestimated.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">So, enjoy Holi with your friends and family.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;"><br /></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;"><b>By Falguni Madlani</b></span></p>
