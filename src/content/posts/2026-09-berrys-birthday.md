<p>&nbsp;</p><h2 style="text-align: center;"><span style="color: #e06666; font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">Berry's Birthday!</span></h2><p align="center" class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: center;"><br /></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">Came back home to a lovely Invitation to attend a birthday party of a stray cat.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">I was pleasantly surprised to see the little kids of my building getting together to celebrate the birthday of a stray cat adopted by my society members during the covid times.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">The kids had elaborately planned for the celebration. They made invites, arranged for the party décor, planned out the menu for themselves and the cat as well!<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">And when all the kids gathered, they also sang the birthday song for Berry, that’s the name given to the cat.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">In any given housing complex, big or small there will always be few people who are against feeding and caring of strays. They get offended at the very sight of these cats and dogs and vey quickly pass a judgement and say “Itna pyaar hain to ghar le jao”. But each to his own.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">There have been instances where the strays have proved a menace to the residents, but they are rare. The animals need love and care more than food.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">I also came across this on twitter which instantly brought a smile onto my face.<a href="https://twitter.com/thebetterindia/status/1401124135561015300?s=20&amp;t=TKJM-yyh9vNipPthdEpqNA">https://twitter.com/thebetterindia/status/1401124135561015300?s=20&amp;t=TKJM-yyh9vNipPthdEpqNA</a><o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">We are born as humans and that is a great responsibility on us because we are the only ones who can help other humans and animals. We should do it as much as we can in our lifetime.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">Let the kids learn sympathy, compassion, love and care at an early age.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">I am not only proud of the kids but also of their parents who despite opposition have not only allowed but also encouraged their kids to love and care for fellow beings.<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">I did enjoy the Birthday Party!<o:p></o:p></span></p><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"><span style="font-size: 12pt; line-height: 17.12px;">&nbsp;By Falguni Madlani</span></p><div class="separator" style="clear: both; text-align: center;"><a href="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgyIn34KJ18flhnVSI4uUf9CFh45iSzJlfjLIRIKplrLdoN5mpSQ_lgs2GMFYaPYRzVerh0NDBx4f3UNRz1dN5yGYB_zdlue3jmN26afdLjKU8nLYO2XXGGCROZ5PQr3iTNeBomKnW0imX02lKE02XkXHbff7sV5wIkE66g247sEO2G0r-JZg4LURFvTw/s1280/WhatsApp%20Image%202022-05-12%20at%206.00.50%20PM.jpeg" style="margin-left: 1em; margin-right: 1em; text-align: center;"><img border="0" data-original-height="1280" data-original-width="1212" height="320" src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgyIn34KJ18flhnVSI4uUf9CFh45iSzJlfjLIRIKplrLdoN5mpSQ_lgs2GMFYaPYRzVerh0NDBx4f3UNRz1dN5yGYB_zdlue3jmN26afdLjKU8nLYO2XXGGCROZ5PQr3iTNeBomKnW0imX02lKE02XkXHbff7sV5wIkE66g247sEO2G0r-JZg4LURFvTw/s1600/WhatsApp%20Image%202022-05-12%20at%206.00.50%20PM.jpeg" width="303" /></a></div><p class="MsoNormal" style="text-align: justify;"></p>
