<p><b>&nbsp;Exams and Questions?</b></p><p>Recently, I saw a Malayalam movie which showed students trying various means to copy. And to top it, the teenager also taught his dad to cheat for his promotional exam. The story line was nice but I was left wondering .....</p><p>Being in the academic field, I regularly come across students who are caught with answers written onto the writing pads, water bottles, etc and lately mobiles and bluetooth devices sneaked inside the exam hall.</p><p>Is the movie influencing the people or the peoples' behavior inspiring the movies.</p><p>Cinema or movies have had a great influence on the people specially the young impressionable minds..</p><p>I remember the time when many friends of mine started emulating Juhi Chawla's way of speech after 'Qayamat se Qayamat tak' was released. There were news reports of couple trying to commit suicide after parents opposing their love as seen in the film.</p><p>Even now teenagers try to model themselves after their favorite stars. With the boom of digital content, there is innumerable protrayal of unethical, irresponsible, superstitious content.&nbsp;</p><p>While I do agree&nbsp; that artistic freedom is essential and it helps the society to grow but&nbsp; it should not come at the expense of promoting negative or harmful behavior.</p><p>Shouldn't media be more responsible and try to shape perceptions, attitude and behavior? The free use of cuss words amongst friends and besties, scenes of drinking, smoking to look cool will definitely cast an effect on the youngsters who may emulate them to look cool.</p><p>&nbsp;Shouldn't we, the consumers of media, remain vigilant and critical of the messages being conveyed? Shouldn't we hold the creators and platforms accountable for their impact on people? Shouln't we ensure that the stories inspire positive change?</p><p>But then again what I may consider as cheating and corruption may not be the same for you for example bribing a traffic cop to let go a parking offence.</p><p>Who decides?</p><p>Media or Us?&nbsp;</p><p>By Falguni Madlani</p>Other Posts:<p><a href="https://falgunimadlani.blogspot.com/2024/01/hurkle-durkle.html" target="_blank">Hurkle Durkle</a><br /></p><p><a href="https://falgunimadlani.blogspot.com/2023/09/my-favorite-ganesha-story.html" target="_blank">My favorite Ganesha story</a><br /></p>
