<p>&nbsp;<b>Friends or ??&nbsp;</b></p><p>&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;Seven long years since they first met.</p><p>&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;Anushri and Kartik had first met during their College exams and were inseparable since then. They shared everything - laughter, tears, worries and food!</p><p>&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;Anushri, the calm and reserved while Kartik, the extrovert complemented each other. They navigated through their college and well into their professions all the while exploring their ideas, thoughts and philosophies.</p><p>&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;In the meanwhile Anu and Kartik stood by each other even during times of difficulty like when Anushri"s father met with an accident. Anushri and Kartik had such a deep bond of friendship that everyone who knew them rooted for "When would they" rather than " Will they or Won't they" kind of situation. It was almost as if destiny had brought them to be together.</p><p>&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;March was eagerly awaited as it was Kartik's birthday month. Both the families started conspiring to make Kartik and Anushri come together. Friends added to the plans. They all decided to convince Anushri to propose going against the tradition. In fact Shreya invoked the grand Irish tradition " that this being a leap year, a girl can propose to a man."</p><p>&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; &nbsp;The D - Day arrived.
