<p>&nbsp;<span style="text-align: center;">HURKLE DURKLE</span></p><p>I learnt a new peculiar and enchanting word today! Yes - you guessed it right.</p><p>"HURKLE DURKLE " It is a 200 year old Scottish term meaning to&nbsp; lounge in bed long after its time to get up and very very apt for these wintry mornings. Well, its never too old to learn new things and words. The word itself feels like a cozy blanket to wrap around yourself.</p><p>And as the winter sets in Mumbai....you can count these few days of pleasant chill on your fingers. So does the time of&nbsp; Annual sports day and cultural fests set in schools and colleges around here. And of course, with February around the corner, exams can't be far behind.</p><p>I extend my warm hugs and sincere appreciation to all the kiddos, students and their parents who instead of hurkling and durkling spend their lovely mornings getting ready for the daily grind. Of course, also not to forget the daily morning walkers, yoga enthusiasts, marathoners who embrace the essence of the season and are fueled by their determination and commitment.</p><p>May this season be a blend of warmth and joy.
