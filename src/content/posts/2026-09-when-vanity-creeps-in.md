<p style="text-align: center;">&nbsp;<strong data-end="163" data-start="134">When Vanity Creeps In...</strong></p><p data-end="626" data-start="165">Just last week, I was speaking to a close friend. It was one of those casual conversation which turns into a rather deep conversation—the kind where you dissect everything from office crisis to family equations, and of course "yeh kaisa jamana aa gaya hai!"&nbsp;</p><p data-end="626" data-start="165">Somewhere in between, I happened to mention a thing done by me.To be honest, I wasn't modest about it—. I said it with a certain emphasis, perhaps even expecting a little admiration in return or maybe with a subconscious need to be acknowledged.</p><p data-end="832" data-start="628">My friend, being the person she is—honest and unfiltered—smiled and gently corrected me.
