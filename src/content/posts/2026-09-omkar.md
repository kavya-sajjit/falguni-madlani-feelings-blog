<p>&nbsp;Yesterday morning, I read an article in the newspaper about a juvenile 10&nbsp; year old, Omkar, separated from his family and running away to Goa. All the authorities concerned and three neighbors have teamed up and are trying very hard to find and unite him with his herd. But, the major concern is that he has temper issues, aggression due to stress and crackers that the farmers were bursting. And on the top of that, he is an Elephant!</p><p>Omkar, the Jumbo strayed away from his herd in Maharashtra and entered into Goa thus starting his adventures or rather misadventures. He needs to be sent back to Maharashtra from Goa and Karnataka's help is required for this as they have "kumki" ( trained ) elephants.</p><p>I just loved this news for once and some how my algorithm does bring these videos of animals behaving like humans in my feed.</p><p><span data-huuid="12323557853920124531">There are many animals who exhibit human like behaviour such as empathy, complex social structures, communication methods and others. <span data-huuid="12323557853920122632">Examples include elephants, monkeys taking care of young ones,&nbsp; using gestures to communicate, dolphins gossiping, showing gratitude, crows using tools to find food.Or is it that we project our human emotions onto these mute helpless creatures ( Anthropomorphism).</p><p>My hubby has a habit of feeding crows from our window. One of the crow seems to have OCD. If he sees a stray piece of food lying around the feeder, he will pick it up, put it in the feeder and then proceed to have his food. Or may be like me, he believes in keeping things in it proper place.</p><p>So, on one hand we have animals behaving like humans and on other hand we have these so called humans behaving like animals.&nbsp;</p><p>Some people have trouble in treating animals humanely.Be it in the&nbsp;&nbsp;wild hunting them for pleasure, game or in the zoos mocking, taunting them or in our societies blaming them for the shit, diseases, and also misfortune.&nbsp;</p><p>Humans are the only ones in this whole solar system who are capable of help to their own kind and other living organisms.</p><p>Time for all of us to start behaving like human and wishing that Omkar is united with his family back.</p><p>Falguni Madlani</p>More posts from this blog:&nbsp;<p><a href="https://falgunimadlani.blogspot.com/2024/03/friends-or.html" target="_blank">Friends-or....
