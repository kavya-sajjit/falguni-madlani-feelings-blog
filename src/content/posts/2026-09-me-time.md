<p>&nbsp;" ME TIME"</p><p>After the usual hustle bustle of a normal regular morning, I was on my way to my college. I boarded my usual train, got a widow seat which made me happy and was on my daily forty five minute commute. I took out my phone and got busy answering the Good morning messages, replying to students, organsing the schedule of the day, deleting junk mails, reading the latest headlines, and so on.</p><p>For some reason, I paused and looked up from my screen.I glanced around and noticed that we were around 16 women sitting in that place and each one of us were glued to our phones including me. It felt as if we all, of different ages, profession, were all hypnotized by that little gadget. We were lost in our own digital life, existing in the same physical space but living in a different world at that moment.</p><p>I started contemplating- " When did this journey - a 45 precious minute of my day— which used to be my " ME TIME" get converted into an addiction, a digital trance.</p><p>As I continued to reflect, I began to understand why I’d been feeling perpetually tired for the past few days. It wasn’t just the hectic schedule or the early mornings; it was the constant barrage of information, notifications,content, I was feeding my brain. My mind was never at rest. I was always available.</p><p>The " ME TIME " during my commute was a chance to reflect, read a book, work on my thoughts, plan my day or speak to my darling mom. It was a time I carved out for myself. But gradually, almost unconsciously, it had turned into shackles,of a mundane screen time.</p><p>Right then and there, I decided that I would reduce my screen time.I need those precious minutes for myself again. I need to see around, read or simply breathe.&nbsp;</p><p>I tried it out the same evening on journey back home. I observed the golden glow of setting sun interspersed with purple hues and let my thoughts run amok. I felt a sense of calm and of course an EGO!&nbsp;</p><p>Sometimes, the best way to recharge is not by trying to do more but by —and for me, that starts with putting the phone down. Hoping that I remain committed to my " Me Time" . A work in progress....What about you?</p><p>By Falguni Madlani</p><p>More Posts:</p><p><a href="https://falgunimadlani.blogspot.com/2025/10/omkar.html" target="_blank">Omkar</a><br /></p>
