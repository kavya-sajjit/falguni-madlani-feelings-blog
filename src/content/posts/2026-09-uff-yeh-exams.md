<div><p>&nbsp;<img src="https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/a/AVvXsEjGmFBRBDjWMwZ8vVSAv2qtlxNq-644eUGtVGf_ryKot_J69zSLbxbJAsRoiG3155HnR-VeA9oMfLMakFHlT4sTMAm6uhH6IoLO8gkZuE9dcvYHuHAFKn8_yB0KLSAERUONODQHtNy45R1bdVymV29MAQTuDteOyXPnu4Sb3J6MW8IVpEwf41f6-K6xpg=s320" /></p></div><p><b>Uff, Yeh Exams!</b></p><p>Exam Season has started. The child and parent that is in my case, me and my daughter have gone into preparation mode. Simple things like buying new pens, pencil pouch have turned into a big event which warrants lots of discussion.&nbsp;</p><p>This year, being a crucial year ( by the way, which year is not crucial?) I have taken leave to help my daughter prepare for her boards. And much of my time is being spent on important discussions like what to make for dinner.😒</p><p>Everything revolves around her exams. We have kept a whole lot of stuff to be done after exams. seeing a movie...after exams, shopping.... after exams, meeting relatives.. after exam, repotting of plants... after exams, seeing places...after exam and what not?&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>And me being at the other end of the spectrum, being a teacher also, I keep on reminding my students of their upcoming board exams and guiding them for their time management. I try to keep them focused on their studies, constantly telling them of how important is the topic of circulation for boards!</p><p>But honestly, Why are we are so concerned about marks? Exams should be there so that the student should know to do their best. They learn the concept of hard work, sincerity and responsibility of completing their work, notes, books while still enjoying their schooling. Instead we have made it so competitive that many of the students lose their enthusiasm and love for learning. Focus should be on developing their problem solving skills, intelligence, confidence and not on Memory.</p><p>And today’s generation doesn’t have it easy.&nbsp; Getting distracted is very easy while focus and determination is difficult to achieve but it only will lead to results. Its not easy for kids to focus during Exams. They have a lot of distractions like Web series, Insta, Netflix , Discord, Facebook.</p><p>And the biggest culprit…mobile phones. Mobiles, surprisingly has legs. It will automatically walk into the hands of the children while they are studying. They don’t take it….</p><p>They use it for studying, reading notes, calculations, seeing time and other stuff...</p><p>What do you say? Do write in the comments section.....</p><p>By, Falguni Madlani</p>
