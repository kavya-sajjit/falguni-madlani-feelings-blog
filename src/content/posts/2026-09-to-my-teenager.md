<p>&nbsp;<span style="text-align: center;">My little Princess</span></p><p>Exactly 18 years ago, you came into our lives and turned it upside down.</p><p>And with you came apprehensions, sleepless nights and worries.</p><p>But we were also rewarded with smiles, cuddles, laughter, shrieks of joy.&nbsp;</p><p>With you I started learning things anew.&nbsp;</p><p>I was surprised at my ability to care for somebody else. I, who was pampered with utmost love and care by my mom and was living without a care till now, suddenly found a fragile doll under my responsibility. The onus of turning you into a good human was on me! I took it up and learnt love, responsibility and fun all along the way.</p><p>I used to be always worried whether I am doing right thing.</p><p>But today when I see you as a pretty young adult ready to take on the world, I am in awe of you. I am often surprised that my little girl who used to hang on my every word, now has the ability to observe, analyse, interpret and stand firmly by her own opinions.</p><p>You possess all those qualities I would personally appreciate in a person. You have turned into a good, kind and a strong individual.</p><p>And I am sure you will conquer your shortcomings if any.</p><p>So, on your birthday wishing you a life full of&nbsp; Happiness, Success and Love.....</p><p>Yours,</p><p>Mama</p><p>Falguni Madlani</p>
